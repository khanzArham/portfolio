## Brief

Landlords and tenants track leases, monthly rent, vacant units and maintenance requests in scattered places. This system brings those records into one structured relational database.

## Method

- **MySQL**, chosen for relational integrity and transactions: foreign keys with cascading actions keep related records consistent, and ACID transactions protect the rent ledger.
- A schema designed from an ERD and normalized to **third normal form (3NF)**.
- **Stored procedures** for the common operations, and **triggers** that keep dependent records in step automatically.

## Failure Log

### Queries slowed sharply on a large test dataset

**What happened:** when we tested with a large dataset, query times rose sharply.

**Why:** the foreign key columns had no indexes, and several queries used nested subqueries, so the database fell back to full table scans.

**What I changed:** I added B-tree indexes to the columns used most often in lookups, and rewrote the nested subqueries as joins, with views for the queries we ran repeatedly.

## Outcome

A working relational system for rental records. I learned relational database design, the trade-offs of normalization, indexing strategies, and how to find and fix slow SQL queries.
